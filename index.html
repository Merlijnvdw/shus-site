FAQ

Will it reply to every message?

No. Shus answers when you mention it, DM it, use a slash command, or in channels you set as AI channels.

Does it remember conversations?

It keeps the last 12 messages per channel. /reset clears them.

Can I turn off the features I don’t need?

Yes. Filters, anti-raid, levels, welcome messages and AI channels are all optional and off until you enable them.
