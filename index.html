Everything your server needs

Turn on only what you want. Everything else stays out of the way.

Moderation

Real mod tools

Warn, kick, ban, timeout, purge, lock and more. Every action checks roles and permissions and is saved as a numbered case.

Protection

Auto-mod

Filters for blocked words, links, invites, spam, caps and mass mentions, with exempt roles. GIF links stay allowed.

Logs

See what happened

Deleted and edited messages, joins, leaves, role and channel changes, voice activity and mod actions, each switchable.

Onboarding

Welcome and autoroles

Custom welcome and goodbye messages with variables, plus separate autoroles for humans and bots.

Roles

Reaction roles and suggestions

Let members pick roles with reactions, and collect numbered suggestions with voting and staff replies.

Community

Levels and starboard

XP for chatting, leaderboards, reward roles with configurable XP, and a starboard for the best messages.

Events

Giveaways and polls

Giveaways with an Enter button that keep running after restarts, plus polls, reminders and timers.

Voice

Music

Play links or searches with a queue, loop, shuffle and volume controls.

Extras

Weather and more

Weather for any city, Twitch, YouTube, Spotify and Last.fm lookups, plus fun commands like 8ball, ship and dice.

All commands

Use them with your prefix, like !ban, or as slash commands, like /ban.
